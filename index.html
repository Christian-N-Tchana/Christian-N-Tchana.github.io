About me

I am an ecologist and PhD researcher at La Rochelle University ( CEBC–CNRS , France), with a particular interest in avian ecology, host–parasite interactions and biodiversity conservation. My research explores how environmental change and human activities influence bird communities, their parasites and associated ecological processes, with a strong focus on agricultural intensification, pesticide exposure and habitat modification. Beyond my doctoral work on farming practices, parasitism and zoonotic pathogens in farmland birds, I am also involved in ornithological research and conservation initiatives in Cameroon, particularly in agricultural and urban landscapes.
