About me

I am an ecologist and ornithologist working at the interface of biodiversity conservation, parasitology and wildlife health. My research combines field-based bird surveys with ecological statistics, laboratory approaches and spatial analyses, primarily in agricultural and urban environments.

My doctoral research at La Rochelle University investigates how organic farming and pesticide use relate to parasitism and zoonotic pathogens in farmland birds. I also contribute to ornithological research and biodiversity initiatives in Cameroon, with particular interests in understudied Afrotropical ecosystems and the training of early-career field researchers.

Research interests

01 / Ecology

Avian biodiversity & conservation

Bird community composition, species diversity and conservation in agricultural landscapes, tropical habitats and urban green spaces.

02 / Disease ecology

Host–parasite interactions

Avian ectoparasites, blood parasites, intestinal parasites and fungal communities, and their ecological associations with host condition.

03 / Environmental change

Agriculture & environmental pressures

How agricultural practices, pesticide exposure and landscape structure may influence bird health and parasite assemblages.

04 / One Health

Wildlife pathogens & zoonoses

Characterising potentially zoonotic agents in wild birds and interpreting their ecology with appropriate attention to transmission evidence.
