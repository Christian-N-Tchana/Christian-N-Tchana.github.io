Research profile

About me

My doctoral research investigates how agricultural practices, particularly pesticide use and organic farming, shape host–parasite interactions and zoonotic risks in farmland specialist birds. By combining field ecology, parasitology, ecotoxicology, and statistical modelling, my PhD research aims to better understand how pesticide exposure influences parasitic infections and potential zoonotic risks.

I hold an MSc in Biodiversity, Ecology and Evolution, specialising in Integrative Biology of Interactions, from the University of Montpellier (France), and an MSc in Zoology from the University of Yaoundé I (Cameroon). My previous research has explored behavioural ecology, parasite responses to environmental conditions, and tropical ecology. Birds are my primary study system.

More broadly, I am interested in disease ecology, conservation biology, ecotoxicology, and how global environmental change affects wildlife health and pathogen transmission dynamics.

Research projects

Current projects

Doctoral research · France

Agricultural practices, parasitism and zoonotic risks in farmland birds

Investigating relationships between organic and conventional farming, pesticide exposure, avian parasite communities, and wildlife health in agricultural landscapes in western France. The work includes intestinal parasites, potentially pathogenic fungi, and other host–parasite systems.

Urban bird ecology · Cameroon

Avian diversity and parasite ecology on the University of Yaoundé I campus

Assessing bird assemblages in an urban university green space and exploring bird-associated parasites. This work contributes to understanding the conservation role of urban green spaces in Central Africa.

Past projects

2024 · UMR MiVEGEC, Montpellier

Environmental stress and transmission strategies in avian malaria

Examined how host stress may affect gametocyte production and sex ratio in Plasmodium relictum (SGS1), using domestic canaries (Serinus canaria) as a model. Supervised by Ana Rivero, Claire Loiseau and Arnaud Berthomieu, with corticosterone-assay collaboration at CEBC.

2023 · UMR CEFE, Montpellier

Ectoparasites, condition and plumage coloration in blue tits

Studied the influence of blood-feeding Protocalliphora larvae on the condition and coloration of blue tits (Cyanistes caeruleus) in Corsica, including possible long-term effects. Supervised by Claire Doutrelant, Claire Loiseau, Amélie Fargevieille and Lisa Sandmeyer.

2019–2020 · University of Yaoundé I

Bird and bat ecology in Lake Ossa Wildlife Reserve

Investigated avian and bat diversity and potential niche overlap in Lake Ossa Wildlife Reserve, Cameroon. Supervised by Sévilor Kekeunou and Eric Moïse Bakwo Fils, with equipment support from IDEA WILD.
